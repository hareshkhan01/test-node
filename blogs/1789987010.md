### What Exactly is MusePad? 🧐

At its core, **MusePad** is a remote-control utility. By installing a small server on your computer and the MusePad app on your Android phone, the two devices connect via your local Wi-Fi network. 

Once connected, your phone’s screen transforms into a massive, sensitive **touchpad**. Moving your finger on your phone moves the cursor on your laptop. It’s essentially like taking the trackpad off your laptop and making it wireless!
